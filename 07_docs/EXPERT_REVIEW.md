## 1. Review Instructions for Agriculture Officer / Agronomist

Please inspect the following JSON knowledge specifications:
1. `03_app/src/config/fertilizer_knowledge.json`: Nutrient focus, fertilizer types, methods, and cautions per condition.
2. `03_app/src/config/application_rules.json`: Weather timing postponement and application rules R1 to R8.
3. `03_app/src/config/advisor-config.json`: Rainfall intensity and wind speed thresholds.

### Key Questions for Evaluator:
1. Are the postponement rules for rain (R1, R2) and wind (R3) consistent with regional best practices in your agro-climatic zone?
2. Is the nitrogen restraint guidance for foliar fungal infections (R7) appropriate for smallholder farmers?
3. Are the plain fertilizer names (e.g., Muriate of Potash, Calcium Nitrate, FYM) clear and readily understood by farmers in your region?
