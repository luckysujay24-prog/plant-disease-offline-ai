## 3. Detailed Profile Audit

### 1. Apple Scab (Venturia inaequalis) (`apple__apple_scab`)
- **Crop**: Apple
- **Fertilizer Role**: Supportive
- **Agronomic Role Explanation**: Fertilizer does not cure apple scab. Balanced nutrition supports canopy recovery and leaf tissue resistance.
- **Nutrient & Soil Focus**: Balanced potassium and calcium; avoid excessive nitrogen.
- **Recommended Organic / Soil Amendments**: Compost, Well-rotted farmyard manure, Potassium sulfate
- **Cultural & Field Sanitation Practices**:
  - Rake and destroy fallen apple leaves in autumn to eliminate overwintering fungal ascospores.
  - Prune tree canopies during dormancy to maximize sunlight penetration and rapid leaf drying.
  - Plant scab-resistant apple cultivars (e.g., Liberty, Prima) where available.
- **Cautions**: Excessive nitrogen fertilizer creates dense, succulent foliage that is significantly more susceptible to scab spore penetration.
- **Dose Guidance**: Follow your soil test report or your local agriculture officer's advice.
- **Citation**: Cornell University Cooperative Extension (2020). Apple Scab Management in Orchards. https://fruit.cornell.edu
- **Verified Date**: 2026-10-11
